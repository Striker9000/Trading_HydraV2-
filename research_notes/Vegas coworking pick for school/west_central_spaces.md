## Q1. Cowork Lounge (3355 Spring Mountain Rd, Unit 62, Chinatown): is it real and open?

### Takeaway
Cowork Lounge's own site and several 2026 directory listings still show it as a coworking space at 3355 Spring Mountain Rd Unit 62. However, the online evidence is thin: a young Facebook page, a small review set that all appears to date from Feb 2026, and a Primerica insurance representative listing the same unit. That fits the user's on-site impression that the unit works mainly as another business's office or training room. It is unconfirmed whether drop-in coworking still runs day to day.

### Cited Findings
- The official site (per search snippet) lists 3355 Spring Mountain Road, Unit 62, Las Vegas, NV 89102, phone (702) 991-WORK / (702) 991-9675, team@coworklounge.com — [Cowork Lounge](https://coworklounge.com/); [daypasscoworking.com](https://daypasscoworking.com/listing/cowork-lounge-las-vegas)
- A Primerica (insurance/financial services) representative also lists "3355 Spring Mountain Rd Unit 62" as an office address — [Primerica rep finder](https://reps.primerica.com/nv/lasvegas/)
- An Eventbrite event, "Career-Ready Applied Generative AI Certification" (Thu Nov 20, 2025), was held at Cowork Lounge, #Unit 62 — [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/career-ready-applied-generative-ai-certification-tickets-1778711587239)
- The Facebook page ID 61563394746499 is the format of pages created around mid-2024 — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/p/Cowork-Lounge-61563394746499/)
- Listing prices: day pass from $25, coworking membership from $280/mo, dedicated desk $400/mo, private office $650/mo. Meeting and event rooms are quoted on request. — [CommercialCafe](https://www.commercialcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/cowork-lounge/); [best-coworking.com](https://www.best-coworking.com/us/nv/las-vegas/cowork-lounge/)
- Amenities listed: coffee/tea, snacks, high-speed internet, projector/TV, podcast room, free monitors for shared-desk members. One listing says 24/7 member access. Another lists Mon–Sat 9am–5pm, closed Sunday. The two conflict. — [CoworkingCafe listing 2](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/cowork-lounge-2/); [CommercialSearch](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/cowork-lounge/)
- Reviews: 5.0 from 8 reviews on directory mirrors, reportedly all from February 2026. They praise the design, natural light, restrooms and nearby food. One directory claims 5.0 from 43 reviews. The counts conflict. — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/cowork-lounge/); [Vegas Cowork directory](https://www.lasvegascowork.com/)
- No Yelp business page with reviews was surfaced. No news coverage of an opening or closure was found.

### Inferences
- The Primerica listing at the same unit, the certification-course event, a small review set that came all at once (Feb 2026), and the conflicting 9–5 vs. 24/7 hours together suggest one of two things. Either the space is shared with, or mainly used by, a financial-services or training business, or coworking is run part-time and by appointment. That matches the user's visit. Treat it as "not verified as a walk-in coworking space."
- Even if it is operating, the cheapest listed membership ($280/mo) is above the $100–200 budget. Only day passes ($25) fit.
- Drive from Spring Mountain & Rainbow: about 10 minutes east on Spring Mountain.

### Gaps
- No access to the live site, Google Maps reviews or Yelp to see 2026 reviews saying "closed" or "not coworking." Recommend calling (702) 991-9675 before visiting.
- Couldn't check Nevada SOS or the Clark County business license for the operating entity.

## Q3. Regus (2300 W Sahara; 500 N Rainbow; Tivoli Village, 410 S Rampart)

### Takeaway
All three are listed as active on Regus.com. Regus is the most reliable route to a sub-$200 plan: listings show a coworking membership "from $129/mo" (a limited-days plan), and Regus advertises hot desks from $25/day. These are real IWG centers with phone booths and meeting rooms. They are business-oriented and quiet, with little community.

### Cited Findings
- Regus Las Vegas general page: hot desking from $25/day, coworking membership from $129/mo. Elsewhere it says coworking desks from $219 per person/mo, with discounts for longer contracts. — [Regus Las Vegas coworking](https://www.regus.com/en/us/nevada/las-vegas/coworking)
- 2300 W Sahara ("Sahara" center): coworking membership from $129/mo, dedicated desk from $235/mo. Another listing says open workspace from $119/mo. — [CommercialSearch – Regus Sahara](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/nv-las-vegas-sahara/)
- 500 N Rainbow: the Regus page shows day coworking from $69/day. An aggregator shows $59/day, $29 half day and $7/hr. — [Regus 500 N Rainbow](https://www.regus.com/en-gb/united-states/nevada/las-vegas/500-north-rainbow-boulevard-2126); [Workin](https://book.workin.space/en/united-states/las-vegas/day-pass)
- Tivoli Village (410 S Rampart): the Regus page shows day coworking from $89/day and offices from $17 per person/day. Aggregators show a coworking membership from $129/mo and a dedicated desk of $235 or $569/mo (they conflict). — [Regus Tivoli Village](https://www.regus.com/en/us/1636); [CommercialSearch – Tivoli](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/nv-las-vegas-tivoli-village/)
- A Sept 28, 2026 Vegas Inc article says IWG (Regus, Spaces, HQ, Signature) is adding eight new centers in Nevada, including one in Summerlin — [Vegas Inc / Las Vegas Sun](https://vegasinc.lasvegassun.com/business/2026/sep/28/why-flexible-coworking-centers-are-poised-to-grow/) (search summary only; the article couldn't be opened)

### Inferences
- In IWG's model, the "$129/mo" coworking membership is typically a limited-days-per-month plan, with business-hours lounge access only and no 24/7. It needs confirming. Regus's own "from $219/mo" desk figure suggests that unlimited use exceeds the $200 budget.
- Drive: 500 N Rainbow about 10 minutes. 2300 W Sahara about 12 minutes. Tivoli about 12–15 minutes.

### Gaps
- Exact plan terms (days per month, after-hours access) and 2025–26 Google reviews for each center weren't found.
