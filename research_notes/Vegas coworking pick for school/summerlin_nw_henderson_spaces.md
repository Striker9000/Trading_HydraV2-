# Coworking spaces in Summerlin, NW Las Vegas, the Southwest and Henderson: status, prices and features (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: This session's network blocks direct page fetches (wework.com, thecoopcowork.com, workinprogress.lv and cooperate.studio all failed to resolve). Everything below therefore comes from web-search result snippets, mostly third-party aggregators (LiquidSpace, Coworker, CoworkingCafe, Yelp, Yellow Pages and directories). Many aggregator prices may be stale. **None of the prices below were confirmed on an operator's live booking page.** Treat them as leads and confirm by phone before paying. Drive times are geographic estimates, not sourced figures.

## Q1: Is each space open and operating as genuine drop-in or member coworking?

### Takeaway
The Coop Summerlin is closed, confirmed by its own site and Yelp. The Coop South (Edmond St) has conflicting signals: the company says it is open, but Yelp flags it as closed. WeWork Two Summerlin, CoOperate at Sunset, Work in Progress (Centennial Hills), Plaza Offices, OFFICENEST, Status, Incubator Space and Corporate Cowork all have active listings, but I could not get recent (2025-2026) proof of operation for any of them. The Innevation Center has no evidence of current drop-in coworking pricing.

### Cited Findings
- **The Coop Cowork Summerlin (1925 Village Center Cir #150): CLOSED.** The Coop's own tour page says "While The Coop Summerlin recently closed, our Edmond location is OPEN at 5955 S Edmond St." — [The Coop tour page (via search snippet)](https://www.thecoopcowork.com/tour/). Yelp lists it as "CLOSED", updated October 2026, with 10 reviews — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-coop-cowork-las-vegas). MapQuest also shows it closed — [MapQuest](https://www.mapquest.com/us/nevada/the-coop-cowork-378158533)
- **The Coop South (5955 S Edmond St): CONFLICTING.** Yelp's page says "CLOSED", updated April 2026, and "Yelpers report this location has closed" — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-coop-cowork-las-vegas-2). The company's tour page says Edmond is OPEN — [The Coop tour page](https://www.thecoopcowork.com/tour/). I could not find a closing date or announcement. The phone number in the listings is (702) 636-9101.
- **WeWork Two Summerlin (10845 Griffith Peak Dr, 89135):** It is listed on WeWork's site and on LiquidSpace — [WeWork](https://www.wework.com/buildings/two-summerlin--las-vegas), [LiquidSpace](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/wework-two-summerlin). The day pass and coworking desk are bookable through WeWork, so it is genuine drop-in coworking — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/two-summerlin-1/). WeWork's Las Vegas pages ran a September 2026 flash-sale banner, which suggests the market page was live in fall 2026 — [WeWork Las Vegas](https://www.wework.com/en-GB/l/coworking-space/las-vegas)
- **Work in Progress, Centennial Hills (6605 Grand Montecito Pkwy):** It has active LiquidSpace listings for an open desk ("50 Available"), a dedicated desk and team rooms — [LiquidSpace open desk](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/work-in-progress-las-vegas-4/office-1-274b), [team room](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/work-in-progress-las-vegas-4/team-room-003), [CommercialCafe](https://commercialcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/6605-grand-montecito-parkway). I found no 2025-2026 news or reviews confirming it is open.
- **CoOperate at Sunset (8400 W Sunset Rd, Suite 300, 3rd floor of the UNLV Harry Reid Research & Technology Park):** It is listed with private offices, conference rooms and dedicated workstations, and LiquidSpace showed "10 Coworking Passes Available." Phone: (702) 900-0440 — [LiquidSpace](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/co-operate-black-fire-coworking-space), [CoOperate site](https://www.cooperate.studio/sunset), [DayPassCoworking](https://daypasscoworking.com/listing/cooperate-at-sunset-las-vegas), [Nevada Business Hub](https://nevada-businesshub.com/resource-profile/cooperate-coworking-studio-sunset-coworking-space). Black Fire Innovation is UNLV's 10,000 sq ft business support and coworking center in the same park, built in partnership with Caesars — [UNLV](https://www.unlv.edu/news/unit/resource-service/857525), [Tech.Vegas](https://tech.vegas/directory/black-fire-innovation/)
- **Plaza Offices (1980 Festival Plaza Dr, Suite 300, Downtown Summerlin):** This is an executive-suites operator that also sells coworking and virtual offices — [Plaza Offices](https://www.plazaoffices.com/), [Coworker](https://www.coworker.com/united-states/nevada/las-vegas/24361-plaza-offices). Vegas Cowork lists it as an open-desk coworking option — [lasvegascowork.com](https://www.lasvegascowork.com/)
- **OFFICENEST (Summerlin, Henderson/Anthem):** Its own application page offers coworking membership at Summerlin and Henderson — [OFFICENEST apply](https://officenest.com/apply/). Deskpass lists OFFICENEST Henderson for **meeting rooms only**, not desk day passes — [Deskpass](https://app.deskpass.com/spaces/officenest-henderson). It is primarily a private-office operator. I found nothing about its E Sahara location.
- **Status Workspace (2651 N Green Valley Pkwy, Henderson):** It is aimed at small and medium businesses and is listed on Coworker and CoworkingCafe — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/henderson/status-workspace/), [Coworker](https://www.coworker.com/united-states/nevada/henderson/status)
- **Incubator Space (3535 Executive Terminal Dr, Henderson):** It is listed with 24/7 access and also books as an event venue — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/henderson/3535-executive-terminal-drive-1/), [Expopass venues](https://venues.expopass.com/venues/incubator-space-henderson-qNd4E7mhGW)
- **Corporate Cowork (Henderson):** It is listed on Vegas Cowork with no reviews — [lasvegascowork.com](https://www.lasvegascowork.com/). A Yelp result for "Corporate Coworking" sits at 5725 S Valley View Blvd, Las Vegas, which may be a different location or a mislabeled listing — [Yelp Henderson search](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Coworking+Space&find_loc=Henderson,+NV)
- **The Innevation Center (6795 S Edmond St):** Switch donated this 65,000 sq ft building and describes it as a "collaborative workspace and event venue." UNLV opened offices there in 2016 — [NV business resource directory](https://www.business.nv.gov/business-resource-hub/resource-directory/entrepreneurial-support/), [Las Vegas Sun, 2016](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2016/jan/29/unlv-opening-offices-at-switch-innevation-center/). I found no current membership pricing or drop-in desk listing.
- **Regus (Downtown Summerlin, 1180 Town Center Dr):** A listing shows "Coworking Membership from $129/month", with the day pass at "Contact for pricing" — [CommercialSearch](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/the-canyons-at-summerlin/), [CommercialCafe](https://www.commercialcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/nv-las-vegas-downtown-summerlin/)

### Inferences
- The Coop's own statement is more authoritative than Yelp for the Edmond location, but the April 2026 Yelp closure flag is a real red flag. Call before going.
- The Innevation Center looks more like an event and corporate venue than a place a student can buy a monthly desk. Treat it as "not confirmed as coworking."
- OFFICENEST, Plaza Offices and Regus are private-office and virtual-office businesses that also sell coworking. Expect quieter rooms but less of a "community" feel.

## Q2: Prices, especially plans at $100-200/month

### Takeaway
The plans most likely to fit $100-200/month are an OFFICENEST Henderson coworking desk at about $150/month and a Regus Downtown Summerlin coworking membership listed "from $129/month." Both figures come from aggregators and are unconfirmed. WeWork day passes (about $39) and Plaza Offices day passes (about $30) work for occasional use. WeWork All Access tiers cost more than $200.

### Cited Findings
- **WeWork Two Summerlin:** Day pass "from $39/day." Dedicated desk from $490/month — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/two-summerlin-1/), [CommercialSearch](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/two-summerlin-1/)
- **WeWork All Access (general, not Las Vegas-specific):** All Access Plus is $339/month, or $289/month on a 12-month plan. Basic is $220/month, or $187/month on a 12-month plan. It is unclear whether Basic includes any Las Vegas building. There is a $10 setup fee, and a Black Friday promotion offers up to 25% off All Access Plus — [WeWork All Access](https://www.wework.com/solutions/wework-all-access?skimoffer=3781658), [WeWork guide](https://www.wework.com/ideas/workspace-solutions/flexible-products/guide-to-wework-all-access)
- **Work in Progress:** Open desk day pass $45/day. Meeting rooms from $20/hour. Virtual office from $79/month. Team room for 4 at $1,399/month. No monthly open-desk price was found — [CommercialCafe](https://commercialcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/6605-grand-montecito-parkway), [LiquidSpace](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/work-in-progress-las-vegas-4/team-room-003)
- **Plaza Offices:** Day pass from $30/day. Coworking membership from $250/month. An older listing showed $350/month. Virtual office from $99/month — [best-coworking.com](https://www.best-coworking.com/us/nv/las-vegas/plaza-offices/), [Plaza Offices](https://www.plazaoffices.com/)
- **OFFICENEST Henderson:** Coworking desk $150/month with a 1-month minimum — [LiquidSpace](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/henderson/prive-offices/hot-desk). No Summerlin price was found. Phone: 702-848-5544 — [OFFICENEST contact](https://officenest.com/contact/)
- **Regus Downtown Summerlin:** Coworking membership from $129/month — [CommercialSearch](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/nv/las-vegas/the-canyons-at-summerlin/)
- **CoOperate at Sunset:** Meeting room for 6 at $50/hour or $350/day. No desk or membership price was found — [LiquidSpace](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/co-operate-black-fire-coworking-space/conference-room-3)
- **Corporate Cowork:** Starts at $29/day — [lasvegascowork.com](https://www.lasvegascowork.com/)
- **Incubator Space:** A reviewer said its rates are "on the lower end" for the area. No figure is given — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/henderson/3535-executive-terminal-drive-1/)

### Inferences
- Regus "from" prices often depend on long contracts. A Regus Edmond, OK listing elsewhere showed $169/month on a 24-month contract, so the $129 Summerlin figure may carry similar terms.
- At $39-45 per day, 3-5 day passes a month would cost about $120-225, which fits the budget for part-time use.

### Gaps
- I found no student discounts anywhere.
- I found no prices for the Innevation Center, Status Workspace, Regus Henderson or Regus Bank of Nevada.
- I could not confirm WeWork On Demand pricing for Las Vegas.

## Q3: Hours and 24/7 access

### Takeaway
Most of these spaces list business hours only. WeWork Two Summerlin and CoOperate list weekdays from about 8 or 8:30 to 5, closed weekends. Incubator Space is the only one listed with 24/7 access.

### Cited Findings
- **WeWork Two Summerlin:** Mon-Fri 8am-5pm, Sat-Sun closed — [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/las-vegas-nv/mip/wework-two-summerlin-548785285), [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/nevada/las-vegas/coworking-space/1339490970-wework-two-summerlin). Member after-hours access is unconfirmed; WeWork monthly members often get keycard access.
- **CoOperate at Sunset:** 8am-5pm or 8:30am-5pm, Mon-Fri. Sources conflict — [DayPassCoworking](https://daypasscoworking.com/listing/cooperate-at-sunset-las-vegas), [Expopass](https://venues.expopass.com/venues/cooperate-at-sunset-las-vegas-hVBjETaxJW)
- **Incubator Space:** 24/7 access — [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/coworking-property/us/nv/henderson/3535-executive-terminal-drive-1/)
- **StartUpNV's International Innovation Center** (downtown, outside the target area): hot desks are available 24/7 by the day or month — [News 3 LV](https://news3lv.com/news/local/startupnv-takes-over-las-vegas-international-innovation-center-expands-coworking-hub)

### Inferences
- A student who studies in the evening may find most Summerlin spaces closed after 5pm unless members get keycard access.

### Gaps
- I found no hours for Work in Progress, Plaza Offices, OFFICENEST, Status, Corporate Cowork or The Coop South.

## Q7: Drive time from Spring Valley (Spring Mountain Rd & Rainbow Blvd), with no traffic

### Takeaway
The closest options are in the Southwest: CoOperate at Sunset, The Coop South and the Innevation Center, all about 10 minutes away. Summerlin is about 15 minutes. Centennial Hills and Henderson are 20-30+ minutes.

### Inferences (geographic estimates, not sourced)
- The Coop South (5955 S Edmond) and the Innevation Center (6795 S Edmond): about 8-12 minutes.
- CoOperate at Sunset (8400 W Sunset): about 10-12 minutes.
- WeWork Two Summerlin, Plaza Offices (Festival Plaza) and Regus (Town Center Dr), all in Downtown Summerlin: about 15 minutes via the 215 or Charleston.
- Work in Progress (Grand Montecito, Centennial Hills): about 20-25 minutes via US-95.
- Status (N Green Valley Pkwy) and Incubator Space (Executive Terminal, near Henderson airport): about 20-25 minutes via the 215.
- OFFICENEST Henderson (Anthem): about 25-30 minutes.

### Gaps
- These times come from map geography and were not checked with a routing tool.
