## 1. Las Vegas-Clark County Library District (LVCCLD) branches near Spring Valley: hours, study rooms, Wi-Fi, recording studios

### Takeaway
The library district is the cheapest base: Spring Valley Library has reservable study rooms (max 2 hours/day per group under April 2026 rules), and the district runs free podcast rooms and media labs, but they are at West Las Vegas (Historic Westside), Enterprise and East Las Vegas branches, not at Spring Valley, and booking generally requires first taking a free intro class.

### Cited Findings
- Spring Valley Library official hours: Mon–Thu 10:00 AM–8:00 PM; Fri–Sun 10:00 AM–6:00 PM; phone (702) 507-3820 — [LVCCLD Spring Valley page](https://thelibrarydistrict.org/locations/SV/); a Las Vegas Sun guide lists different hours (Mon–Thu 9 AM–9 PM) — [Las Vegas Sun libraries guide](https://lasvegassun.com/guides/about/public_libraries/). Official district page should be trusted; the Sun guide is likely outdated.
- District study room guidelines (revised April 1, 2026): one reservation per group per day, up to 2 hours; stays beyond 2 hours not permitted; "stacking" reservations by multiple members of the same group not permitted; cancel through link in confirmation email; room lights must stay on — [LVCCLD Study Room Guidelines (2026 PDF)](https://thelibrarydistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/54/2026/05/Study-Room-Guidelines.pdf)
- Older (2021/2022) guideline version said a room could be released if the group did not arrive within 10 minutes — [LVCCLD Study Room Guidelines (2022 PDF)](https://thelibrarydistrict.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/54/2022/01/Study-Room-Guidelines.pdf) (older; may not be current)
- Computer reservations are handled via a district reservation page — [LVCCLD Reserve a Computer](https://thelibrarydistrict.org/reserve-computer/)
- West Las Vegas Library (new building, Historic Westside, opened ~Dec 2025) has podcast studios, video recording rooms, a Podcast Room and an "Innovation Lab" (Room 158) where patrons edit episodes using Descript; its "Westside Studio" multimedia lab includes podcasting studios, recording rooms, rendering and music production equipment, and a green screen — [LVCCLD news: New West Las Vegas Library opens](https://thelibrarydistrict.org/news/new-state-of-the-art-west-las-vegas-library-opens-its-doors/); [LVCCLD West Las Vegas About](https://thelibrarydistrict.org/wlv_about/); [Las Vegas Weekly, Dec 18 2025](https://lasvegasweekly.com/news/2025/dec/18/las-vegas-historic-westside-welcomes-new-library/)
- Fox5 (March 4, 2026) reported West Las Vegas Library offers free creative tech resources to the community — [Fox5 Vegas](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/03/04/west-las-vegas-library-offers-free-creative-tech-resources-community/?outputType=amp)
- Recurring "West Las Vegas Podcasting 101" and "Podcasting Playbook" (Foundations / Distribution) classes, plus "Basic Podcast Editing" — [LVCCLD event 16956657](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/16956657); [Podcasting Playbook: Foundations](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/16587226); [Basic Podcast Editing](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/17183389)
- Enterprise Library has a "Digital Memories Preservation Lab & Podcasting" space; its class covers basics of podcasting equipment and grants access to the podcasting spaces — [LVCCLD Introduction to Podcasting](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/12588825)
- East Las Vegas Library has a "Built from Scratch Multimedia Lab" and Podcast Room; a 6:00–6:30 pm beginner session teaches equipment and, once completed, patrons can book the podcast room (event pages include a direct room-booking link for those who already took the class) — [LVCCLD event 9858572](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/9858572); [LVCCLD event 11566146](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/11566146)
- District programs are described as free and open to the public — [LVCCLD event 9858562](https://events.thelibrarydistrict.org/event/9858562)
- Windmill Library is tagged on Yelp as "Good for working, Wifi available" with a free adult computer lab — [Yelp Spring Valley laptop-friendly search](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Laptop+Friendly+Cafe+Free+Wifi&find_loc=Spring+Valley%2C+NV)
- UNLV Lied Library has its own multimedia production spaces (for UNLV affiliates) — [UNLV Libraries Multimedia](https://www.library.unlv.edu/spaces/multimedia)

### Inferences
- For interviews/video calls, a 2-hour Spring Valley study room booking is workable for single calls, but cannot serve as an all-day private office (no stacking).
- Enterprise Library (southwest valley) is likely the closest branch with a podcast space for a Spring Mountain & Rainbow resident; West Las Vegas has the most complete studio (video + green screen) but is a longer drive.
- Recommended workflow: take the free intro podcast class once, then book podcast rooms for recording sessions at no cost.

- Could not confirm whether Windmill, Sahara West or West Charleston have podcast/media labs (search showed only unconfirmed hints for Windmill, nothing for the others).
- Library card requirement, per-session time limits, and how far in advance podcast rooms can be booked: not found.
- Library Wi-Fi speeds / session limits: no source found.

## 2. Free or cheap entrepreneur workspaces & resources (SBDC, SCORE, IIC, Black Fire, CSN, chambers, Black-focused incubators)

### Takeaway
The strongest budget fit is the International Innovation Center @ Vegas (downtown, now run by nonprofit StartUpNV): $150/month unlimited hot desk, plus free "Co-Working Fridays." Nevada SBDC and SCORE give free advising but not desk space; UNLV Black Fire Innovation (8400 W. Sunset Rd, ~SW valley, close to Spring Valley) has CoOperate coworking day passes via LiquidSpace. No Black-owned coworking space was found; a Black-community-focused business hub (Historic Westside Innovation Commons) is under construction.

### Cited Findings
- StartUpNV took over management of the International Innovation Center (IIC) at 300 S. 4th St. #180, downtown, in May 2026; offers coworking, business address, mail forwarding/scanning, and a free entrepreneur resource library — [Nevada Business Magazine, May 2026](https://nevadabusiness.com/2026/05/startupnv-takes-over-management-of-international-innovation-center-in-downtown-las-vegas/); [News3LV](https://news3lv.com/news/local/startupnv-takes-over-las-vegas-international-innovation-center-expands-coworking-hub); [Warren Group LV](https://thewarrengrouplv.com/startupnv-takes-over-management-of-international-innovation-center-in-downtown-las-vegas/)
- IIC pricing per StartUpNV: hot desk $150/month (unlimited days); dedicated desk $250/month; small private office $1,000/month; large private office $2,995/month; conference room $500 half-day / $1,000 full day; nonprofits 50% off event space — [StartUpNV IIC page](https://startupnv.org/iic/). Third-party directory lists "from $25/day" — [Vegas Cowork directory](https://www.lasvegascowork.com/)
- StartUpNV hosts "Free Co-Working Fridays in Downtown Las Vegas" at IIC (multiple Luma instances; one dated Oct 2); no free parking lot—metered street and adjacent paid garage — [Luma listing](https://luma.com/abk3xcuk); [Luma listing 2](https://luma.com/lr17lx12). Fabulous Nevada describes free coworking on the first Friday of each month — [Fabulous Nevada](https://fabulousnevada.com/community/nonprofit-of-the-month-startupnv/). (Conflict: "every Friday" naming vs. "first Friday" description — verify.)
- Nevada SBDC: free, confidential one-on-one advising and workshops; offices include UNLV Research Park, 8400 W. Sunset Rd., Las Vegas 89113, and North Las Vegas (2250 Las Vegas Blvd. N.) — [UNLV SBDC services](https://www.unlv.edu/sbdc/services); [Nevada SBDC training calendar](https://nevadasbdc.org/training-calendar/list/)
- SCORE Las Vegas: free mentoring, low/no-cost workshops and free webinars; serves Las Vegas through Pahrump — [SCORE Las Vegas](https://www.score.org/lasvegas/about); [SCORE workshops](https://www.score.org/nv/las-vegas/business-education/)
- UNLV Black Fire Innovation (Harry Reid Research & Technology Park) houses CoOperate coworking, RebelForge, a Makerspace, and on-site Nevada SBDC — [UNLV Black Fire Innovation](https://www.unlv.edu/econdev/black-fire-innovation); [Makerspace at Black Fire](https://unlv.edu/econdev/bfmakerspace)
- CoOperate at Black Fire sells day passes on LiquidSpace (3rd-floor suite, kitchen/lounge access, refund if cancelled 24h ahead); price not captured — [LiquidSpace CoOperate Black Fire](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/co-operate-black-fire-coworking-space/day-pass)
- RebelForge provides no-cost business advising/training (UNLV student-oriented) — [UNLV RebelForge](https://www.unlv.edu/econdev/rebelforge)
- Note: "Black Fire" is a UNLV/Caesars brand name, not a Black-focused program — [UNLV news on Black Fire](https://www.unlv.edu/news/article/unlv-black-fire-innovation-fueling-southern-nevadas-growing-tech-ecosystem)
- Historic Westside Innovation Commons (formerly Westside Microbusiness Park), Clark County-funded, operated with UNLV Office of Economic Development; opening scheduled early 2027 (another source said 2026) — [Business in Clark County](https://businessinclarkcounty.com/growth-developments/historic-westside-innovation-commons/); [Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/53m-government-funded-las-vegas-microbusiness-park-breaks-ground-3324028/); [Scofield Group, Aug 2026](https://www.scofieldgroup.com/blog/2026/8/11/historic-westside-comeback-microbusiness-park-las-vegas)
- Separate Historic Westside entrepreneurial training center (MYS Firm with City of Las Vegas) broke ground ~May 2026 — [Las Vegas Black Image Magazine](https://lasvegasblackimage.com/2026/05/historic-westside-entrepreneurial-center-breaks-ground/)
- Urban Chamber of Commerce (Las Vegas): office at 1951 Stella Lake St., Suite 26, 89106; Christopher Shelton named president/CEO (Aug 2026) — [VegasInc Q&A](https://vegasinc.com/business/2026/aug/03/new-chamber-of-commerce-leader-emphasizes-access-f/); [VegasInc places](https://vegasinc.lasvegassun.com/places/urban-chamber-of-commerce/)
- Nevada state entrepreneurial-support directory — [business.nv.gov](https://www.business.nv.gov/business-resource-hub/resource-directory/entrepreneurial-support/)

### Inferences
- IIC hot desk ($150/mo) fits the $100–200 budget and comes with a startup community, but it is downtown (~20–25 min drive from Spring Mountain & Rainbow) with paid parking.
- Black Fire / UNLV Research Park on W. Sunset Rd. is geographically the closest "innovation hub" to Spring Valley and co-locates free SBDC advising.

## 3. Student discounts and national day-pass apps

### Takeaway
No student discounts were found at WeWork or Regus. Day passes in Las Vegas run roughly $25–$40/day through LiquidSpace and WeWork; WeWork's monthly plans exceed the budget unless promotional.

### Cited Findings
- WeWork Las Vegas (Town Square, Two Summerlin): day passes from $39/day; monthly memberships from $289/month (cached page) — [WeWork Las Vegas](https://www.wework.com/l/coworking-space/las-vegas)
- WeWork All Access Plus listed at $225/mo + tax (undated page); $10 one-time setup fee; Black Friday promo up to 25% off All Access, redeemable Nov 17–29, 2026 (not student-specific) — [WeWork All Access](https://wework.com/solutions/wework-all-access); [WeWork 25% offer](https://wework.com/info/25-percent-offer-on-your-wework-membership)
- LiquidSpace Las Vegas day passes: Pacific Workplaces (300 S. 4th St.) $25/day; Corporate Coworking hot desk $29/day; Incubase Workspace $30/day essential, $40/day premium — [LiquidSpace Pacific Workplaces](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/pacific-workplaces-las-vegas/open-desk-1); [LiquidSpace Corporate Coworking](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/space-cowork); [LiquidSpace Incubase](https://liquidspace.com/us/nv/las-vegas/incubase-workspace/dedicated-desk-day-pass-for-7)
- Incubator Space (Henderson, 3535 Executive Terminal Dr.) offers day passes, memberships, dedicated desks — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/incubator-space-henderson)

### Inferences
- At ~$25–39/day, 4–6 day passes/month fit $100–200, but an unlimited IIC hot desk ($150) is better value for frequent use.

### Gaps
- No Deskpass Las Vegas presence/pricing found. Croissant: no current info found (status unknown). Regus/Industrious student offers: none found.
