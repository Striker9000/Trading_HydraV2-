FAQ
Real questions

    
Do I need a license or a phone plan?
No. The radios use a free, license-free band (915 MHz in the US). No SIM card, no monthly bill for the radio.

    
How far does it reach?
Pocket radios reach roughly half a mile to two miles in the city. Every radio passes messages along, and rooftop Towers stretch it across whole neighborhoods. The more of us, the farther it goes.

    
Is it private?
Messages are encrypted, so outsiders with radios can't read them. Anyone who has the channel code can, though. Treat the main chat like a big group text and don't post anything you wouldn't want screenshotted. Location sharing comes switched off.

    
What happens in an emergency?
Trusted operators in each city post verified alerts on Bleshtastic, tagged like [OP-SLA1 20:42]: what to avoid, where help is, and when it was last checked. If it doesn't come from a known operator, double-check it before you share it.

    
Can I just use the Meshtastic app?
The Meshtastic app needs a radio to talk to. With no radio, use Phone Mode through the Bleshtastic chat room instead.
