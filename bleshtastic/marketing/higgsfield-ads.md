# Bleshtastic × Higgsfield: AI video ad scripts

Five vertical (9:16) ads, each broken into shots. Every shot has a **start-frame prompt** (make the image first) and a **motion prompt** (animate it). Text, logos, the QR code and voiceover get added afterward in an editor (CapCut), never by the AI.

---

## How to make these in Higgsfield

1. **Build the cast once.** Use Higgsfield's character / consistent-face feature (Soul ID or whatever it's currently called) to make 3 **original** characters so they look the same in every ad. Never use real people or celebrities.
   - **Dre**: 30s, barber, fade with a part, clean white tee, gold chain, easy smile
   - **Keisha**: mid-20s, braids in a high bun, oversized jersey, hoop earrings
   - **Ms. Lou**: 70s, silver curls, cardigan, reading glasses on a beaded chain, sharp eyes
2. **Make the start frame** for each shot as an image (9:16) with the start-frame prompt. Regenerate until it's right.
3. **Animate it** with image-to-video using the motion prompt. Higgsfield has camera-move presets. The preset named in each shot is the move to look for, so pick the closest one if names differ.
4. **Film the real device yourself.** AI can't draw a T1000-E, Pager or T-Deck accurately; it'll invent buttons. Shoot 3–5 second phone clips of the real thing (pulling the card out of a wallet, the pager buzzing, thumbs on the T-Deck) and cut them in. These real shots also make the ad believable.
5. **Edit in CapCut:** captions, the Bleshtastic wordmark, the end card (use `print/out/sticker.png`, which has the working QR), music, and voiceover.
6. **Voiceover:** your own voice or a friend's beats any AI voice for this audience. Record on your phone in a closet (clothes kill echo).

### Style line (paste at the end of every start-frame prompt)
> cinematic 35mm photo, vertical 9:16, Black American neighborhood, natural skin tones, warm golden light with deep plum shadows, purple and gold color grade, shallow depth of field, real textures, no text, no logos, no watermark

### Rules that keep you out of trouble
- **Label it.** TikTok, Instagram and YouTube want realistic AI video marked as AI-generated. Turn on their AI label when posting.
- **No fake emergencies.** Don't generate realistic riot, police or disaster footage. It reads as fake news and will get flagged. Blackouts, crowded events, dead zones and family moments sell it fine.
- **Phone screens:** have the AI show phones from behind or at an angle, then put the message overlay on in CapCut. AI-made screen text is gibberish.
- **Hands:** keep hands short in AI shots (typing is where AI breaks). Use your real device footage for close-ups of hands.
- Say "works with Meshtastic," never imply Bleshtastic *is* Meshtastic.

## Posting plan (first 4 weeks)
- **Week 1:** Ad 1 + Ad 4. Pin Ad 1 to the profile.
- **Week 2:** Ad 5 (comedy spreads furthest) + a real behind-the-scenes clip of you setting up a T1000.
- **Week 3:** Ad 2 + a real street test: walk away from a friend until it stops delivering, show the distance.
- **Week 4:** Ad 3, timed with a storm, heat wave or a big event in your city.
- Mix real footage in: every AI ad should be followed by a real-device post so people trust it.
- Reply to every comment asking "how do I get one?" with a video reply. Those replies are free ads.
