## Setup card (goes in every box)

**Welcome to Bleshtastic.**
1. **Charge it** (USB-C, about 2 hours).
2. **Get the app:** search "Meshtastic" in the App Store or Google Play.
3. **Pair it:** open the app → tap **+** → pick **Blesh XXX-0000** (the name on your label). PIN, if asked: on the device screen, or `123456`.
4. **Done.** Your Bleshtastic channels are already loaded. Say what's up in **Bleshtastic**.

Rules of the network:
- Alerts are trusted only with an operator tag like `[OP-SLA1 20:42]`.
- Don't post addresses, plans, or anything you wouldn't want screenshotted.
- Don't share your QR code online. Invite people face to face.
- Lost your device? Tell your ambassador so we can rotate keys.

## Short-video scripts (15–30s, TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

**1. "Airplane mode"**
Close-up of a phone: airplane mode ON, Wi-Fi OFF. Pull a card out of the wallet.
Type "where y'all at?" → send. Cut to a friend across the park, whose pager buzzes: "by the grill."
Text on screen: *No service. Still delivered.* → **BLESHTASTIC**

**2. "Stadium / festival"**
Crowd shot. VO: "Forty thousand people, zero bars." Show a "Message failed" on a normal text.
Same message on Bleshtastic goes through. *Find your people anyway.*

**3. "Grandma test"**
Hand the Pager to an elder. "Read it to me." She reads: "Dinner at 6, bring ice." She hits reply. Laughs.
*If grandma can use it, you can.*

**4. "What's in the box"**
Unboxing. Card, cable, setup card. "Charge. Pair. Done. That's it. That's the whole tutorial."

**5. "Host a Tower"**
Barber on the roof with a small solar box. "This right here covers the whole block. Cookout, outage, whatever. We're connected."

## Emergency alert templates (operators only)

Keep alerts under ~200 characters so they fit in one mesh packet.

```
[OP-SLA1 20:42] ALERT South LA: Avoid Crenshaw & Slauson, road closed. Safe spot: [PLACE]. Next update 21:15.
[OP-SLA1 20:42] SHELTER open: [PLACE, ADDRESS]. Water, charging, first aid. Until 06:00.
[OP-SLA1 20:42] MEDICAL help at [PLACE]. For emergencies call 911 if lines work.
[OP-SLA1 20:42] ALL CLEAR [AREA]. Roads open. Verified 20:40.
[OP-SLA1 20:42] RUMOR CHECK: "[claim]" is NOT confirmed. Don't repeat it until we verify.
```
**ES:** `[OP-SLA1 20:42] ALERTA: Eviten [LUGAR]. Lugar seguro: [LUGAR]. Próxima actualización 21:15.`
**HT:** `[OP-SLA1 20:42] ALÈT: Evite [KOTE]. Kote ki an sekirite: [KOTE]. Pwochen nouvèl 21:15.`
**FR :** `[OP-SLA1 20:42] ALERTE : Évitez [LIEU]. Lieu sûr : [LIEU]. Prochaine mise à jour 21h15.`

## Outreach list (first city)
- Barbershops & salons: counter card plus a demo device. Offer a free Tower to the first 3 shops.
- Churches: announce after service, run a signup table, put a Tower on the steeple or roof.
- HBCU / college Black student unions, NPHC chapters.
- Block clubs, neighborhood councils, mutual-aid groups, CERT teams.
- Events: Juneteenth, cookouts, car meets, festivals, homecoming. Demo where the cell network jams.
- Local Black-owned radio/podcasts: "the group chat that works in a blackout."

## Ambassador kit (one per neighborhood)
- 5 provisioned Cards + 1 Pager demo unit
- 50 counter cards and 50 setup cards
- Access to the operator Alerts room
- Script: what it is → live demo → who vouches → how to buy
