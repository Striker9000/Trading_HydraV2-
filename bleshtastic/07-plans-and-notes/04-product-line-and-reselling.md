# 04 · Product line, branding and reselling

Model: **value-added reseller.** Buy the devices, load the Bleshtastic setup, box them in your own packaging, sell kits.
People pay for it working out of the box, already on the Block, with someone to call.
*Product names, prices and manufacturer custom-order programs are from memory. Confirm with sales before planning around them.*

## Three levels of branding
| Level | What | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Branded kit (**start here**) | Stock device + preloaded channels + your box, setup card, sticker | Legal right away |
| 2. Skinned device | Custom vinyl skin or case | Don't cover the FCC label. Don't open or modify the radio |
| 3. True white label | The factory makes it with your logo and colors | Seeed, LilyGo and RAK take custom orders, usually with minimums in the hundreds or thousands |

**Rules:** don't claim you made the hardware ("Bleshtastic Card, built on the SenseCAP T1000-E"). Don't modify radio hardware (that breaks FCC approval). Don't use their logos as yours.
**When selling for real:** an LLC, sales tax, product liability insurance, and lithium battery shipping rules.

## Kits
| Kit | Contents | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| The Card | T1000-E, preloaded, setup card, sticker | "No extra gadget" person |
| The Pager | LilyGo Pager, preloaded, quick-reply buttons | Elders, simple users |
| The Communicator | T-Deck, preloaded | Power users |
| The Tower | Solar outdoor node, set up as a relay, mounting hardware | Lights up a street |
| **Block Starter** | 3 Cards + 1 Tower | "Cover my family and my block." Likely the best seller |
| **Blackout Kit** | Card or Pager + crank/solar power bank + emergency info card | Storm and outage season |

## Features beyond a stock device
- The Block preloaded. Named devices registered to the buyer.
- **Quick-reply buttons** on the Pager: "I'm safe," "Need help," "Need water," "Power's out here," "On my way" (Meshtastic's preset-message feature).
- Key Maker card for vetted Cookout buyers.
- Opt-in tracker mode on a private channel (a kid's backpack, an elder who wanders).
- A printed "When the power goes out" card, plus real human setup support.

## Blavor B007 (crank/solar power bank)
You can't rebrand it, but you can: (1) bundle it in the Blackout Kit, (2) use an affiliate link, or (3) pitch Blavor on a partnership or co-branded bundle.
**Test first:** time how much hand cranking it takes to send a few messages on a T1000-E before claiming it in an ad.

## The viral video: real, not AI
- **"72 hours, no power, no cell. Can I still reach my people?"** A daily series living off the Blavor and the Bleshtastic devices.
- "I cranked this for 60 seconds and texted my mom with zero bars." Only if it tests true.
- "Building a neighborhood network for $200." Sells the Block Starter.
- **Honest claims:** it can't call 911, and range depends on Towers. Say "stay connected when the towers can't." Don't exploit real tragedies.

## Order of moves
1. Buy 5–10 T1000-Es + 1 solar node at retail and build your own Block Starter.
2. Film the 72-hour series with them.
3. Sell the first 20–50 kits by DM or a simple shop page and see which kit sells.
4. Then wholesale pricing from Seeed and LilyGo, a Blavor partnership, and maybe custom-logo devices.
